🏮 Original · Bahasa Indonesia

Suatu sore, Budi datang ke sebuah penginapan yang terletak di desa terpencil tempat ia ditugaskan selama seminggu kedepan. Pak Andi sang pemilik penginapan mengantarnya ke kamar sambil memperingatkannya agar tidak keluar kamar malam-malam karena penginapan tersebut sering diganggu makhluk halus, namun Budi tertawa dan berkata, “Saya nggak percaya begituan, Pak! Daripada hantu, saya lebih takut lihat saldo rekening bank saya di akhir bulan.” Pak Andi pun ikut tertawa lalu berkata, “Pak Lurah bilang besok pagi ada jam 8:00 di halaman, ikut ya.” Budi langsung bertanya, “Maksudnya bagi-bagi apel gratis, Pak?” Pak Andi hanya tersenyum sambil menggelengkan kepala.

Setelah mereka menyusuri lorong-lorong yang kosong dan tiba di depan kamar, seketika Budi bercanda, “Malam ini rame ya Pak, kayak di kuburan, hahaha!” Pak Andi tertawa dan menjawab, “. Sepi itu namanya. Ya sudah, segera masuk kamar dan istirahatlah.” Lalu Budi pun masuk ke kamar dan tertidur, tetapi sekitar pukul 3 pagi ia terbangun karena mendengar suara-suara aneh dari luar kamarnya. Saat ia keluar untuk memeriksa keadaan, disitu ia melihat Pak Andi berdiri di samping seekor kucing yang sudah mati berlumuran darah. Dengan kaki yang tidak menapak lantai dan mulut yang dilumuri darah, Pak Andi perlahan menoleh kearahnya, sambil tersenyum lebar ia berkata, “ juga kamu ya, kan sudah dibilang jangan keluar kamar.”

👻 DeepL · English

One afternoon, Budi arrived at an inn located in a remote village where he had been assigned to work for the next week. Mr. Andi, the inn’s owner, showed him to his room while warning him not to go out at night because the inn was often haunted by spirits. However, Budi laughed and said, “ I’m more afraid of seeing my bank balance at the end of the month than I am of ghosts.” Mr. Andi laughed too and said, “The village chief said there’s an ‘’ at 8:00 a.m. tomorrow in the courtyard—be sure to come.” Budi immediately asked, “You mean they’re handing out free apples, sir?” Mr. Andi just smiled and shook his head.

After they walked through the empty hallways and arrived in front of the room, Budi immediately joked, “It’s crowded tonight, isn’t it, Sir? Just like a cemetery, hahaha!” Mr. Andi laughed and replied, “ That’s what you call quiet. All right, go inside your room and get some rest.” Then Budi went into his room and fell asleep, but around 3 a.m. he woke up because he heard strange noises coming from outside his room. When he went out to check what was going on, he saw Mr. Andi standing next to a dead cat covered in blood. With his feet not touching the floor and his mouth smeared with blood, Mr. Andi slowly turned toward him. With a broad smile, he said, “You’re such a —I already told you not to leave the room.”

🕯️The story in four scenes

  • 🌆Budi arrives at a remote village inn for a week of work.
  • 🍎Pak Andi mentions an apel. Budi hears free fruit.
  • 🚪“Don’t leave your room at night.” Budi laughs it off.
  • 🐈‍⬛3 a.m.: a bloody cat, and a smiling Pak Andi whose feet don’t touch the floor.

🔦Read between the lines

Question: Was the village chief going to give out free apples at 8:00 a.m.?

No. The village chief wasn’t going to give out free apples. Pak Andi was talking about apel pagi, which means morning assembly or roll call. Budi misunderstood the word apel as the fruit.

🕸️Conclusion

By comparing the translations, we can see that the DeepL can translate most of the sentences correctly, but it sometimes translates individual words too literally and it still struggles with idioms, double meaning words and cultural expressions. However, a human reader, especially those who understands the language can recognize the meaning behind the expressions (such as, ‘kepala batu’, ‘apel’, and ‘Rame dari Hong Kong’) to get the jokes and sarcasm to be able to understand and enjoy the story. Therefore, understanding a language is not only about translating words by words and a translation that seems correct might not carry its original context, idioms, and tones.
